# Governance

ECHO is maintained by its repository owner (@Venuss1337), who makes final
decisions on scope, design and releases and explains them in issues, pull
requests or architecture decision records (`docs/ADR`).

- Changes are proposed as pull requests and merged after review and green
  checks.
- Decisions that a future contributor might reopen are recorded as ADRs.
- Releases follow [docs/RELEASE.md](docs/RELEASE.md).
- Security reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); conduct reports follow
  [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

As regular contributors emerge, review responsibilities will be shared so
that no single person is required to approve every change.
