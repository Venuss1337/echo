# Code of conduct

ECHO is a small volunteer project. Everyone taking part, maintainers
included, is expected to be respectful and constructive.

## Expected

- Assume good faith, and ask before concluding.
- Keep criticism about code, designs and decisions, with reasons.
- Respect people's time and boundaries; nobody owes unpaid work.
- Credit others' work and respect licenses.
- Keep other people's private information, files and logs private.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, threats, slurs, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
publishing someone's private information, deliberate disruption, and
repeated hostility after being asked to stop.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainer (@Venuss1337) using the
contact options on their GitHub profile, or through GitHub's
[report abuse](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
tools. Do not put incident details in public issues.

Maintainers will review reports fairly and confidentially and may respond
with a warning, removal of content, or a temporary or permanent ban from
project spaces, in proportion to what happened. A maintainer involved in a
report does not decide it alone.
